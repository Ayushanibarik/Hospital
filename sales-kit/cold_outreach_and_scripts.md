## 2. Discovery Call Script (Section AF)

* **Step 1: The Opening (Frame the Call):**  
  *"Before I show you anything, I want to understand where the patient journey currently breaks in your clinic."*

* **Step 2: Diagnosis Questions (Quantify Leakage):**
  * *"How many new patient inquiries do you receive each month across WhatsApp, website, and ads?"*
  * *"What is the typical response time during busy OPD hours or after 7 PM?"*
  * *"What percentage of confirmed appointments end up as no-shows?"*
  * *"How does your staff currently re-engage patients who don't show up?"*
  * *"What happens after a patient is discharged—who checks in on their recovery?"*

* **Step 3: The Pain Isolator:**  
  *"If you could remove one manual coordination process from your reception team tomorrow, what would it be?"*

* **Step 4: Reflect & Demonstrate:**  
  Reflect back their exact words:  
  *"You mentioned reception is overwhelmed answering routine questions while doctors are sitting with empty slots from no-shows. Let me show you how our automation layer solves that exact issue."*  
  *(Open `http://localhost:3000/` and show the 2-minute live demo).*

* **Step 5: Transition to Pilot:**  
  *"If this worked exactly as shown, where would this save your team the most time or recover the most appointments?"*

* **Step 6: Recommend Pilot & Close:**  
  *"Rather than overhauling your entire clinic at once, we recommend starting with a 3-week pilot on [Specific Workflow] for ₹50,000. Would you like us to start with this highest-leakage workflow?"*

## 3. Objection Handling Battlecards (Section AJ)

### Objection 1: "We already have an HMS / EMR system."
* **Response:** *"Good! We do not replace your HMS. Think of this as an automation and communication layer around it. Your HMS holds the records; our layer eliminates the manual WhatsApp messaging, reminder calls, and no-show follow-ups so your staff doesn't have to."*

### Objection 2: "Our front-desk staff can do this manually."
* **Response:** *"Absolutely, and they are probably doing their best. But during peak clinic hours, answering repetitive WhatsApp messages pulls them away from in-clinic patients. Our goal is to handle 80% of repetitive coordination instantly so your staff can focus on in-person care and exceptions."*

### Objection 3: "₹1,00,000 is too expensive."
* **Response:** *"The ₹1,00,000 package is for multiple connected departments, discharge tracking, and custom executive reporting. For clinics starting out, we offer the ₹50,000 Starter Pilot focused purely on one measurable problem—like recovering your missed appointments. If that saves even 15–20 consultations a month, the system pays for itself immediately."*

### Objection 4: "Can you guarantee a specific revenue increase?"
* **Response:** *"No responsible healthcare technology partner should promise a revenue guarantee before analyzing your baseline numbers. What we do commit to is measurable operational KPIs: reducing first-response time to under 1 minute, achieving a 98% reminder delivery rate, and recovering a documented percentage of no-shows."*

### Objection 5: "Can you integrate with our specific HMS?"
* **Response:** *"I can confirm that as soon as we review your vendor's API or webhook documentation and permissions. If your vendor doesn't have open APIs, we can also use automated scheduled handoffs so your workflow is still automated without compromising security."*
