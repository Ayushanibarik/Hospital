### Tips for Recording
1. **Pre-load both tabs:** Have `http://localhost:3000/` open on Tab 1 and `http://localhost:3000/admin` open on Tab 2.
2. **Use Loom or OBS:** Record in high resolution (1080p minimum).
3. **Pacing:** Keep the demo snappy—let each screen action take 3 to 5 seconds.
