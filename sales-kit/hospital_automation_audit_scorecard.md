# Hospital Automation Audit & Scorecard Framework
*Section AB & AC of Hospital AI Automation Master Blueprint V3*

The **Paid Discovery Audit** is the wedge product sold to hospital administrators to uncover operational leakage before pitching full automation implementation.

---

## The 7-Step Paid Discovery Process

```
Step 1: Stakeholder Interviews (Owner, Medical Director, Reception Lead, Billing, IT)
  │
Step 2: End-to-End Patient Journey Mapping (From first ad click to post-discharge)
  │
Step 3: Inventory Existing Systems & Manual Handoffs (HMS, WhatsApp phones, Paper logs)
  │
Step 4: Quantitative Leakage Measurement (Missed calls, lead drop-off %, no-show rate)
  │
Step 5: Score Automation Opportunities (Impact × Frequency × Feasibility ÷ Risk)
  │
Step 6: Select 1 Core Pilot Workflow (Starter implementation)
  │
Step 7: Deliver 3-Page Executive Audit Report + Implementation Proposal
```

## Opportunity Prioritization Formula

$$\text{Workflow Priority Score} = \frac{\text{Revenue Impact (1-5)} \times \text{Frequency (1-5)} \times \text{Technical Feasibility (1-5)}}{\text{Operational / Clinical Risk (1-5)}}$$

### Pilot Recommendation Threshold:
* **Score $\ge 25$:** Prime candidate for **Starter ₹50,000 Pilot** (Usually Lead Intake $\rightarrow$ Auto Confirmation $\rightarrow$ No-Show Recovery).
* **Score $15 - 24$:** Phase 2 Expansion candidate (Discharge check-ins, Diagnostic alerts).
* **Score $< 15$:** Keep manual until core automation baseline is proven.
